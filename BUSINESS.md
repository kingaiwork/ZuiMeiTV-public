# ZuiMeiTV — Creator Intelligence Opportunity

## English

The creator economy produces enormous amounts of public information, but discovery and evaluation remain fragmented. ZuiMeiTV’s opportunity is to organize that public surface into a more useful creator-intelligence and media product.

### Potential customer value

- discover creators earlier;
- understand public momentum more clearly;
- compare relevant creator categories;
- prepare better brand or media research;
- give creators a clearer view of their public presence.

### Commercial paths

Premium reports, brand research, campaign discovery, creator services, sponsorship, media products and strategic collaborations are possible directions as the product matures.

### Trust principle

Public influence data should be contextualized. Scores are decision aids, not universal judgments about a person’s worth or quality.

## 中文

创作者经济产生大量公开信息，但发现、比较和研究仍然很分散。ZuiMeiTV 的机会是把这些公开信息组织成更有价值的创作者情报和媒体产品。

长期可以探索高级报告、品牌研究、活动发现、创作者服务、赞助、媒体产品和战略合作。所有评分都应该有背景和解释，只能作为参考，不能代表一个人的绝对价值。
