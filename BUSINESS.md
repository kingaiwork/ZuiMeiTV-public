# ZuiMeiTV · Business & Partnership Direction

## English

ZuiMeiTV focuses on a practical creator-economy opportunity: public creator information is fragmented, difficult to compare and often mixed with opaque rankings or paid placement.

### Commercial directions

Potential future paths include verified creator profiles, premium reports, brand discovery, media kits, campaign intelligence, selected data/API products, clearly labeled sponsorship and creator-business services.

### Strategic value

A creator platform can become more useful as its public data history, verification signals, corrections and trend records accumulate over time. The long-term opportunity is to become a trusted discovery and intelligence layer rather than a pay-to-rank list.

### Partnership interests

Relevant conversations may include creators and agencies, brands, media platforms, public-data providers, creator-economy technology, research partners and long-term strategic investment.

No revenue, user-count, partnership or market-leadership claim is implied unless explicitly verified.

# 中文

ZuiMeiTV 面向一个真实的创作者经济问题：公开创作者资料分散、难以比较，而且很多榜单容易与不透明排名或付费展示混在一起。

### 商业方向

未来可以探索创作者认证档案、高级报告、品牌发现、媒体包、营销智慧、部分数据/API 产品、明确标识的赞助以及创作者商业服务。

### 战略价值

随着公开数据历史、验证信息、纠错记录和趋势不断积累，创作者平台可以越来越有价值。ZuiMeiTV 的长期机会是成为可信的发现与智慧层，而不是“付钱就能上榜”的名单。

### 合作方向

适合的合作方向包括创作者及经纪机构、品牌、媒体平台、公开数据服务、创作者经济技术、研究伙伴及长期战略投资。

除非经过明确验证，本文件不代表已经取得收入、用户规模、正式合作或市场领先地位。
